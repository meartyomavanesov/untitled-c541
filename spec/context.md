The Personal Finance Manager is a robust mobile application designed to help individuals and families take control of their financial health. Targeting users who want to manage their budgets, track expenses, and achieve financial goals, the app allows users to input their income and expenditures, categorize transactions, and create personalized budgets. Users can set savings goals, monitor their spending habits through detailed reports, and receive alerts for bill payments and budget limits. By providing a clear overview of their finances, the Personal Finance Manager empowers users to make informed decisions, reduce debt, and build wealth over time.

- Budget creation and management tailored to individual needs
- Expense tracking with categorization for better insights
- Goal setting for savings and debt reduction
- Visual reports and analytics to monitor financial health
- Alerts for budget limits and upcoming bill payments
- User-friendly interface for easy navigation
- Secure data protection to ensure privacy and safety
- Integration with bank accounts for real-time transaction updates
